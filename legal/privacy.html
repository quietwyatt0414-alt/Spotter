SPOTTER

Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

The short version. Spotter stores your workout log so it can show it back to you on your phone and laptop. It has no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't sell or share your data. It does count a few anonymous daily totals, like how many workouts get logged, to see what's working. If you sign in (with Google, Apple or an email and password), your log is stored in Google's Firebase cloud, where the person who runs Spotter can see it. If you use Spotter without an account, your log never leaves your device. If you buy Spotter Pro, Stripe handles the payment; we never see your card details, and we keep only a record that your account has Pro.

Who runs Spotter

Spotter is a free workout tracker run by Witwire (Wyatt Whitaker), an individual developer based in California, USA. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean that person. You can reach us at quietwyatt0414@gmail.com.

Two ways to use Spotter

  • Without an account. Your log is saved only in your browser's storage on that device. We never receive it. Clearing your browser data, or losing the device, erases it, so use Settings → Copy backup now and then.
  • Signed in with Google, Apple, or an email address and password. Your log is saved to your Spotter account in Google Firebase, so it syncs between your devices. The rest of this policy is mostly about this option.

Payments

When you tap Buy Pro, Spotter sends Stripe your account ID and the email address on your Spotter account (so Stripe can send your receipt), and you're taken to a checkout page run by Stripe. There, Stripe collects your payment details and anything it needs to process the payment and prevent fraud, such as your card, billing country or postal code, and device information. Stripe handles that under its own privacy policy, and is responsible for it as the payment processor. Stripe then tells Spotter whether the payment succeeded, which is how Pro gets unlocked. The owner can see your payments (amount, date, email and the last digits of the card, as Stripe shows them) in the Stripe dashboard, to issue refunds and keep records.

Bodyweight and health information

Your workouts and bodyweight say something about your fitness and health. We treat them as personal information, use them only to run the app for you, and never use them for advertising or share them. Spotter isn't a medical service and isn't covered by HIPAA.

Where your data is stored

Signed-in data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), in data centers in the United States. Firebase also keeps a copy on your device so the app works with bad signal at the gym; signing out of Spotter clears that copy.

Spotter's security rules let each signed-in person read and write only their own log. Other people using Spotter can't see your data.

Who can see your data

  • You, on any device where you sign in.
  • The person who runs Spotter. As the owner of the Firebase project, we can view, export, change and delete any user's data in the Firebase console. The app's privacy rules don't apply to the owner. The owner also reads feedback messages and the daily totals on a private stats page. We only look at your workout log when we need to: to fix a bug or a sync problem, to answer a request from you, to delete your data, or if the law requires it. Nobody else has admin access today.
  • Google, which hosts and processes the data for us as our service provider under its own data processing terms.
  • Stripe, if you buy Spotter Pro, as described under Payments above.
  • Authorities, only if we're legally required to share it, or if it's needed to protect someone's safety.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Other services the site loads

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase (from gstatic.com and Google's sign-in pages), to sign you in and sync your log. Firebase Authentication also handles email sign-in and sends password reset emails, and Firebase Cloud Functions, which start a checkout and receive Stripe's payment confirmations.
  • Stripe, only if you choose to buy Spotter Pro: its checkout page (checkout.stripe.com) collects your payment. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Sign in with Apple (Apple's sign-in pages), if you choose it. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts, for the app's typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

Cookies and device storage

Spotter doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's local storage and IndexedDB to keep you signed in, keep an offline copy of your log, remember small things like which quote you saw last, and (without an account) store your whole log. The site also installs a service worker so it can open without a connection.

How long we keep it

We keep your log and usage bookkeeping for as long as you have a Spotter account. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), your sign-in record and usage bookkeeping are deleted right away. We keep your workout log, plus your name and email so we know whose it is, for 30 days in case you change your mind, and then delete them for good. Feedback messages are kept until we've dealt with them, and you can ask us to delete yours. The anonymous daily totals can't be traced back to you, so we keep them. If you bought Spotter Pro, the purchase record on your account follows your log: it's deleted when your log is deleted for good. Stripe keeps its own records of payments, and we keep payment records from the Stripe dashboard (amount, date and the email on the receipt) for as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually up to 7 years. Google may keep its service logs and backups for a limited time under its own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: everything is in the app, and Settings → Copy backup gives you a full copy you can keep.
  • Correct it: edit or delete any workout, exercise or weigh-in in the app.
  • Erase your log: Settings → Delete all data clears your workouts, routines and custom exercises from your account. Your account and sign-in record stay until you delete your account.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account deletes your sign-in right away and signs you out. Your log is kept for 30 days, then deleted for good.
  • Change your mind: within those 30 days, sign in again and email quietwyatt0414@gmail.com from the email address of your old account, and we'll move your log into your new account.
  • Delete it sooner: email us and we'll permanently delete your log without waiting the 30 days. You can also email us if you can't use the button.
  • Stop syncing: sign out, or use Spotter without an account.

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), you may have legal rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, and to object to how it's used. Email us to use any of them; we won't treat you differently for doing so. We rely on running the service you asked for as our legal basis for processing. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS and is stored in Google's infrastructure, and Spotter's database rules block anyone but you (and the owner, as described above) from your log. No system is perfectly secure, though. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Children

Spotter isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child under 13 has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account. If you're under 18, check with a parent or guardian before using Spotter.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant (for example, if we add ads, analytics or paid features that use your data differently), we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
