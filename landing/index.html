Last time, right next to this time

Every set shows what you did last session, so you always know the number to beat.

  • Previous sets pre-filled. Hit the check if you matched it, or bump the weight.
  • Warm-up sets marked separately, so they don't mess up your PRs.
  • Rest timer starts when you finish a set.
  • Your record for the lift sits at the top: weight × reps and estimated 1RM.
  • Swap the variation (barbell, dumbbell, machine, Smith) without losing history.

See every session at a glance

A calendar of every day you trained, with volume, time and every set you did.

  • Day streaks that keep you showing up.
  • Sick or on vacation? Mark the day and your streak survives.
  • Monthly totals: days trained, workouts and pounds moved.

Know if you're actually progressing

With Pro, the Progress tab answers the only question that matters for each lift.

  • Progressive overload: yes or no, in plain English.
  • A target for next time, like "try 275 × 7 or 280 × 6".
  • Charts for estimated max, heaviest set and volume.
  • Changed gyms? Fix the numbers when a new machine reads heavier or lighter.

Questions

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Logging, routines, history, PRs, streaks, warm-ups, imports and backups are free with no limits and no ads. Pro is an optional one-time $19.99 for the Progress tab and spreadsheet export.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Chalkbook runs in your browser on any phone or computer. To make it feel like an app, add it to your home screen: on iPhone, tap Share then "Add to Home Screen"; on Android, tap the menu then "Install app".

Do I need an account?

No. Tap "Use without an account" and your log stays on that device. Sign in with Google any time to sync it between your phone and laptop, and your log moves over.

Who can see my data?

Nobody else using Chalkbook can see your log. There are no ads and no third-party trackers, and your data is never sold. Chalkbook only counts anonymous totals, like how many workouts get logged each day. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Chalkbook imports exports from Strong, Hevy and Liftoff, plus any CSV with dates, exercises, weights and reps.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Once it's on your home screen it opens and logs offline, then syncs when you reconnect.

Who makes Chalkbook?

Chalkbook is made by Witwire, an independent developer in California, not a big company. Ideas and bug reports go straight to the person building it through Settings → Send feedback.
